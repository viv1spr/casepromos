"Free case", "no deposit bonus", "free skins code" — almost every CS2 case site promises something for nothing. Some of it is real, but the conditions decide whether the bonus is worth anything. Here is how these offers usually work.

## What a promo code gives you

When you sign up through a promo code, the site credits a small welcome bonus. Depending on the site it can be:

- **a free case** or a few free spins,
- **a small balance** to play with,
- **a deposit bonus** — extra percent on your first top-up.

Only the first two are truly "no deposit". The deposit bonus needs you to pay first.

## The usual conditions

Free bonuses almost always come with rules. Typical ones:

- **Steam account requirements** — a minimum Steam level or a public profile, so the site knows you are not a bot.
- **Withdrawal limits** — items won with bonus money often can't be withdrawn until you deposit or wager a certain amount.
- **One bonus per person** — multiple accounts get banned.

The exact terms are always on the site itself, usually right after you enter the code. Read them before you play.

## How not to lose money

- Treat case opening as **paid entertainment**, not as a way to earn — the expected value is below what you pay.
- Set a budget before you deposit and stop when it is gone.
- Never chase losses with a bigger deposit.
- Case and roulette sites are for **18+** only, and in some countries they are not legal — check before you sign up.
