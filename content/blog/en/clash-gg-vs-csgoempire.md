## Clash.gg — for case openers

Clash is built around opening cases and case battles, where several players open the same cases and the best total drop wins. Roulette is there too, but it is a side mode. Deposits are mostly crypto-based, and withdrawals are flexible: crypto, cash, and even physical items.

## CSGOEmpire — roulette plus a skin market

CSGOEmpire is best known for its roulette, but its biggest practical advantage is the **P2P market**: you can deposit and withdraw skins directly with other users instead of trading with site bots. Games use a provably fair system, so results can be verified, and the site operates under a Curaçao license.

## Which one should you choose?

- **You want cases and battles** — Clash.gg.
- **You want to deposit and withdraw skins** — CSGOEmpire, thanks to its P2P market.
- **You only use crypto** — both work; Clash also offers cash and physical-item withdrawals.
- **Licensing matters to you** — CSGOEmpire operates under a Curaçao license.

## Before you sign up

Both sites are gambling and are for **18+** only. Online gambling is restricted in many countries — check your local laws first. Set a budget, never chase losses, and treat it as paid entertainment, not income. If you only want skins, a [P2P marketplace](/en/p2p-marketplaces/) is usually the cheaper way to get them.
