Steam Market money can't be withdrawn, so if you want real cash for your CS2 skins you need a third-party P2P marketplace. DMarket, Waxpeer and LIS-SKINS are three of the most used ones. All three let buyers pay 0% — the difference is in the seller fee and in how you get your money out.

|---|---|---|---|

| Buyer fee | 0% | 0% | 0% |

## DMarket — lowest fee

With a **2% seller fee**, DMarket is the cheapest of the three to sell on. It also has instant bot trades besides the marketplace. The catch: money from sales is locked for 7 days before you can withdraw it, so it is better for planned sales than for quick cash.

{{site:dmarket}}

## Waxpeer — PayPal payouts

Waxpeer charges sellers 6%, but it is one of the few markets that pays out to **PayPal** (or Steam balance). Some users report payout delays, so plan bigger withdrawals in advance.

{{site:waxpeer}}

## LIS-SKINS — fast crypto

LIS-SKINS takes 5–6% from sellers and pays out in crypto — and its crypto withdrawals are among the fastest on the market. If you are comfortable with crypto, it is the quickest way to turn skins into money.

{{site:lis-skins}}

## Which one to use

- **Lowest fee, not in a hurry** — DMarket.
- **Want the money on PayPal** — Waxpeer.
- **Want it fast and use crypto** — LIS-SKINS.
