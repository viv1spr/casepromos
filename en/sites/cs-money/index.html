How to use CS.MONEY: Trade, Market and Instant Sell

Trade mode is for swapping: you put your skins on one side, pick skins from the bots on the other, and the trade goes through instantly. The site's commission is built into item prices, so an equal-value swap has no separate fee.

Market mode works like a classic P2P marketplace. Sellers pay 5% (3% for items priced from $1,000), buyers pay no fee, and listings from other players are often cheaper than in Trade mode.

Instant Sell lets you sell a skin for balance right away when you don't want to wait for a buyer — handy, but usually a bit cheaper than a Market sale.
