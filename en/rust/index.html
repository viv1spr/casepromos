FAQ

Where can I sell Rust skins for real money?

P2P marketplaces such as DMarket, Waxpeer and LIS-SKINS let you list Rust skins and withdraw the balance. Bot-trading sites (Tradeit, SkinSwap) buy instantly but usually pay a little less than a P2P sale.

Do CS2 promo codes work for Rust on the same site?

Usually yes: a promo code or referral bonus is tied to your account, not to one game. Always check the bonus terms on the site itself.

How do I trade Rust skins safely?

Turn on the Steam Guard Mobile Authenticator, only confirm trades you started yourself, check the bot's Steam level and name in the confirmation, and never log in to Steam through links from chats or DMs.

Learn more: guide to CS2 skin sites

How Rust skin sites work

Rust items are regular Steam inventory items, so third-party sites can accept them through Steam trade offers. Some sites let you trade Rust skins for other Rust or CS2 items, others pay out the value as balance you can withdraw.

Which type of site to choose

  • P2P marketplace: best price when selling, but the sale can take some time.
  • Bot trading: instant swap or sale from a large bot inventory, at a small spread.
  • Case sites: entertainment for 18+ only — treat it as spending, not as income.

Tips before you deposit Rust items

  • Compare the price offered for the same skin on two or three sites.
  • Check withdrawal methods and minimum amounts before depositing.
  • Keep your Steam API key private; revoke it if you shared it by mistake.
