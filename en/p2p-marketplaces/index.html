FAQ

How do I use a promo code?

Click “Get bonus”, sign up on the site (usually via Steam), then enter the promo code in the bonus or deposit section.

Are free cases really free?

Yes, most sites give a free case or balance for new users with a code. Withdrawing winnings may require a minimum deposit — check each site's rules.

Is it legal?

What is a P2P skin marketplace

On a peer-to-peer marketplace you buy skins directly from other players. The site acts as a middleman: it holds the payment until the trade is completed and charges the seller a commission.

How to compare marketplaces

  • Seller fee (from about 2% to 15%) and any buyer or withdrawal fees.
  • Liquidity: how many listings there are and how fast items sell.
  • Payout methods for selling skins for real money.
  • Trade protection and support in case of disputes.

Tips

Compare the price of the same skin on several marketplaces before buying, and check the float and pattern of expensive items.
