FAQ

How do I use a promo code?

Click “Get bonus”, sign up on the site (usually via Steam), then enter the promo code in the bonus or deposit section.

Are free cases really free?

Yes, most sites give a free case or balance for new users with a code. Withdrawing winnings may require a minimum deposit — check each site's rules.

Is it legal?

It depends on your country. Only use licensed sites and only if you are 18 or older.

What are CS2 case opening sites

Case opening sites let you open custom cases with CS2 skins and withdraw your drops to your Steam inventory. Besides cases, many of them offer case battles, upgrades and contracts.

How to pick a case site

  • A provably fair system that lets you verify every opening.
  • Drop chances and item prices shown for every case.
  • Fast and reliable skin withdrawals.
  • Free cases and promo codes for new users.

Play responsibly

Opening cases is gambling: on average you get back less than you spend. Set a budget, take breaks and play only if you are 18+ and it is legal in your country.
