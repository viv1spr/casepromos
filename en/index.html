FAQ

How do I use a promo code?

Click “Get bonus”, sign up on the site (usually via Steam), then enter the promo code in the bonus or deposit section.

Are free cases really free?

Yes, most sites give a free case or balance for new users with a code. Withdrawing winnings may require a minimum deposit — check each site's rules.

Is it legal?

It depends on your country. Only use licensed sites and only if you are 18 or older.

How to choose a CS2 skin site

There are hundreds of CS2 skin sites today: marketplaces where players buy and sell skins, bot trading sites for instant swaps, instant-sell services that pay out real money, case opening sites and roulette games. This catalog collects them in one place with promo codes, bonuses and the key facts you need before signing up.

What to check before signing up

  • Reputation and age of the site: prefer long-running platforms with active support and community reviews.
  • Fees: on marketplaces the seller fee usually ranges from about 2% to 15%; buyers often pay nothing extra, but deposit and withdrawal fees may apply.
  • Payment methods: card, crypto, PayPal or skins — make sure you can both deposit and withdraw the way you prefer.
  • License and terms: case and roulette sites should state their license and rules; read the withdrawal conditions before depositing.
  • Country availability: some sites are not available in certain countries.

How CS2 promo codes work

Most sites reward new users who sign up with a promo code: a free case, free balance, a deposit bonus or a better trade rate. Copy the code from the card, open the site with the “Get bonus” button and enter the code in the bonus or deposit section. Bonuses often come with wagering or minimum deposit rules, so check them first.

Trading vs. case opening

Trading sites and marketplaces let you buy, sell and swap skins at market prices — the value stays in your inventory. Case opening and roulette sites are games of chance: on average you get back less than you spend, so treat them as entertainment, set a budget and never chase losses. They are for adults 18+ only.

Staying safe

  • Sign in only through the official Steam login page and never share your Steam Guard codes or API key.
  • Double-check every trade offer in the Steam app: the partner, the items and the price.
  • Beware of fake sites with look-alike names — open sites only from trusted links.
