FAQ

Where can I sell Dota 2 items?

P2P marketplaces such as DMarket, Waxpeer and LIS-SKINS list Dota 2 items and let you withdraw your balance. Compare fees and withdrawal methods before you sell.

Do promo codes work for Dota 2 items too?

On multi-game sites a promo code or referral bonus is linked to your account, so it normally applies whatever game you trade. Check the terms on the site.

How do I avoid scams when trading Dota 2 items?

Use the Steam Guard Mobile Authenticator, confirm only trades you started, verify the trade partner in the confirmation screen, and ignore 'free item' links in Steam chat or Discord.

Selling and buying Dota 2 items outside the Steam Market

Steam Market balance cannot be withdrawn as cash. Third-party P2P marketplaces let players sell Dota 2 items to each other and withdraw the money, usually for a seller fee of a few percent.

What to compare

  • Seller and buyer fees.
  • Withdrawal methods: card, crypto or e-wallets.
  • How fast the item is delivered and whether a trade hold applies.
